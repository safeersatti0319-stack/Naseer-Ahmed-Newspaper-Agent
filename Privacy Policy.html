Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Learn how we handle and protect your personal information.

📅 Last Updated: 2026

At Naseer Ahmed Newspaper Agent, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to keep it secure. By using our services, you agree to the practices described below.

01 Information We Collect

To provide reliable newspaper delivery, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name and contact details (phone number, WhatsApp)
  • Delivery address and area location
  • Newspaper and magazine subscription preferences
  • Payment-related information for subscription management

02 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for service-related purposes:

  • Processing and managing your newspaper subscriptions
  • Delivering your newspapers and magazines on time
  • Responding to your inquiries via phone or WhatsApp
  • Sending updates about your subscription or service changes

03 Information Sharing

We respect your privacy and never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared if:

  • Required by law or legal authorities
  • Necessary to complete your delivery service (e.g., delivery staff)
  • You have given explicit consent

🔒 Your data stays with us. We do not engage in marketing campaigns or share your details with advertisers.

04 Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. While no system is 100% secure, we continuously strive to maintain the highest standards of data safety.

05 WhatsApp Communication

By contacting us via WhatsApp, you consent to communication through the platform. Please note that WhatsApp has its own privacy policy governing data handling. We recommend reviewing their policy for complete transparency.

06 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to active subscriptions)
  • Withdraw consent for communication at any time

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
